# In memory of Lala

Dear Lala,

We remember your bright smile and your love for snacks, especially Snacks Swingg. You brought so much joy to our lives with your happy energy and playful spirit. Your passing has left a big hole in our hearts, but we know you're now at peace.

Though you're no longer with us, your memory will live on in our hearts and in the love we share with each other. We'll keep you in our thoughts and prayers, and we'll celebrate your life with all the love and happiness we can give.
